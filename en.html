FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED.

How much is a transfer from Split Airport to the city center?

The fixed price from Split Airport to Split center is €35 for a vehicle up to 4 passengers. No hidden fees — fuel, tolls, parking and VAT included.

Do you operate at night and on weekends?

Yes, available 24/7 including overnight flights, weekends and holidays. No night surcharges.

What if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight in real time. If it's delayed — we wait, no extra fee. If it's cancelled, just let us know and we'll reschedule.

How do I pay?

Payment is after the ride. Cash (EUR) or card. Invoice (R1) available for business clients.

Can I get a child seat?

Yes, child seats (booster and infant) are available on request, free of charge. Just mention the child's age when booking.

How much luggage fits?

3 large suitcases + 2 smaller bags for 4 passengers. For more or special items (bikes, surfboards), let us know in advance.
