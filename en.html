Frequently asked

Have a question?

How much is a transfer from Split Airport to the city center?

The fixed price from Split Airport to the center is €35 for a vehicle up to 4 passengers — no hidden fees. Fuel, tolls, parking and VAT included.

Do you operate at night and on weekends?

Yes, available 24/7 including overnight flights, weekends and holidays. No night surcharges.

What if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight in real time. If it's delayed — we wait, no extra fee. If cancelled, just let us know and we'll reschedule.

How do I pay?

Payment is after the ride. Cash (EUR) or card. Business invoice (R1) available.

Can I get a child seat?

Yes, child seats (booster and infant) are available on request, free of charge. Just mention age when booking.

How much luggage fits?

3 large suitcases + 2 smaller bags for 4 passengers. For more or special items (bikes, surfboards), let us know in advance.
